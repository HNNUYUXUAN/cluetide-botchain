Our release commitment

  • Every content change creates a new version and requires a new reviewer approval.
  • Authorize one publication attempt.
  • Keep the delivered evidence.

Delivery evidence

  1. Original files and the executor signature: check package consistency.
  2. Confirmed chain receipt: check the approved version and execution attempt.
  3. Downloaded artifact: compare its bytes with the approved file.

File equality and the executor statement do not establish business quality. Tests and human review assess quality.
